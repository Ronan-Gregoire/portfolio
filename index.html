CPGE Research Project 2025 — 2026

Study of Solar Cycles via the Babcock-Leighton Dynamo Model

As part of my TIPE (Travaux d'Initiative Personnelle Encadrée) during my preparatory classes, I conducted a thorough scientific investigation into solar magnetic activity cycles. The core of this research focused on implementing and analyzing the Babcock-Leighton dynamo model, which describes how poloidal and toroidal magnetic fields regenerate each other within stellar convection zones.

By combining differential rotation, meridional circulation, and magnetic flux emergence simulations, this project allowed me to bridge rigorous mathematical differential equations, astrophysical concepts, and computational scripting to model cyclical solar magnetic phenomena and examine butterfly diagram characteristics.
